# Engineering result interpreter

Separate offline-analysis, soft-sensor candidate, and closed-loop-control readiness. Cite the evidence behind every gate, include blockers and required validation, and avoid production or safety claims unsupported by external operating periods, measurement traceability, and online monitoring.
